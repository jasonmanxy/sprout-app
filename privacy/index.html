Sprout: One Second a Day ("Sprout", "the app") is made by jiacheng wei ("we", "us").

The short version

The app does not collect anything. It has no user accounts, no servers, and makes no network requests. Your videos, photos, notes and settings stay on your iPhone. We cannot see them, and neither can anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything the app creates is written to the app's own private storage area on your device (the iOS application sandbox):

  • the video clips and photos you record or import
  • the thumbnails generated from them
  • the date each one belongs to, an optional one-line note, and which album it belongs to
  • your settings (beauty level, filter, reminder time, clip length, and so on)

None of this is uploaded. There is no cloud backup, no synchronisation, and no copy held by us.

One clarification, because it is the question people ask most: the app does not exclude its files from your iPhone's own backup. If you use iCloud Backup, or back your iPhone up to a computer, those backups are made by iOS and include app data, so restoring a new iPhone from one brings your days across. Those backups belong to you and to Apple; we have no access to them and no part in making them.

What we collect

Nothing.

We do not collect personal data, usage data, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, contacts, or location. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party SDK of any kind. It does not open network connections.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

Camera

Why
To record your daily one to three seconds, or take one photo. Frames are processed on your device's GPU and written to the app's own storage.
If you say no
You cannot record inside the app. You can still add days from your photo library.

Microphone

Why
To record sound along with the video, if you leave "Record sound" on. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
If you say no
Videos are recorded without sound.

Photo Library (read)

Why
Only when you choose to add a day from your library. The app reads the single item you picked and copies it into its own storage. It does not browse or index your library.
If you say no
You cannot import from your library. Recording still works.

Photo Library (add only)

Why
Only when you tap "Save to Photos" after making a memory film. The app writes one video file. It does not read your library with this permission.
If you say no
The film is not saved to Photos. You can still share it with the system share sheet.

Notifications

Why
To send the daily reminder, if you enable it. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server; there is no push service involved.
If you say no
No reminders. Everything else works.

You can review or revoke any of these in iOS Settings → Sprout.

Face detection

The beauty feature uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to find the position of a face in the current camera frame, so that smoothing can follow the face. This runs entirely on your device, in memory, frame by frame. No face data is stored, no face is identified or recognised, and nothing leaves the device. Turning the beauty switch off in the app's settings stops it.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose your data, because we do not have it.

When you tap "Save to Photos" or "Share", iOS takes over: the resulting video is handed to Apple's Photos app or to the app you pick in the system share sheet. What happens to it after that is governed by that app's own privacy policy, not by ours.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age. Parents who use it to record a child's growth should know that everything they record stays on their own device.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single day: open it and tap the bin icon.
  • Delete an album and everything in it: Settings → tap the album → Delete this Seed.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Data → Erase everything.
  • Delete everything, including the app: uninstall the app. iOS removes the app's entire storage area with it.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing for us to restore. If a memory matters to you, export the film and save it to your Photos library first.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, APPI, PIPA and similar laws)

These laws give you the right to access, correct, export, delete, and object to the processing of your personal data. They apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. All of your content is already in your own possession, on your own device, and you can delete it yourself at any time using the steps above. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it stores or sends, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above before that version ships. As long as the app makes no network requests, this policy will not materially change.
