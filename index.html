Sprout

One second a day.
In a year, a film.

Sprout is an iPhone app for recording one to three seconds of your day — or taking a single photo — and letting the days join themselves into a film you can watch whenever you like.

There is nothing to edit and nothing to upload. You open it, hold the shutter, and close it again. The film already exists; it just gets one day longer.

What it's for

Things that change too slowly to notice, and are startling to watch back. A baby learning to sit, then stand, then run. A plant on the windowsill through four seasons. Yourself, after the run, every evening.

One day on its own is worth almost nothing. Four hundred of them in a row will stop you where you stand.

How a day works

  1. Tap or hold. A tap takes one photo. Holding records up to three seconds. Shoot another if you want — the previous take waits underneath and you can swap back with one tap.
  2. Miss a day, fill it in. Every empty date has a + on it, however far back. Pull something in from your photo library; for a video you drag along a filmstrip to choose which seconds to keep.
  3. Watch it, or export it. Press play any time. Export the reel as a video to your Photos library, or hand it to whoever it's for.

It never leaves your phone

No account. No sign-in. No cloud. No analytics. No ads. No third-party code of any kind. Sprout makes no network requests at all — not for backup, not for crash reports, not for anything.

That cuts both ways, and it is worth knowing before you start: delete the app and it is all gone, and nobody has a copy to hand back to you. Export the films that matter.

Face smoothing, if you use it, runs on this phone's GPU. Face position is found with Apple's on-device Vision framework, frame by frame, in memory. Nothing about your face is stored or sent anywhere. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Good to know

  • RequiresiPhone, iOS 17 or later
  • OrientationPortrait only
  • Recorded at1080 × 1920, every clip
  • CostNo subscription, no in-app purchases
  • AlbumsAs many as you like, each with its own film, reminder and day one
  • RemindersOptional, per album, and skipped on days you've already recorded
